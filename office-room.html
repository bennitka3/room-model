Office fit check

    Guessed, not measured

    • Office: built to your tape measure. The closet opening and door position come from the floor plan; the chair, glass cabinet and canvases are sized from photos.
    • Rest of the house: room sizes are the plan's printed figures. Door and window positions are read off the drawing, good to a few inches. Ceilings are drawn at 96″.
    • Fixtures and furniture outside the office: placed and sized by eye from your photos. They show the house as it is today, minus the keyboard and guitars, which the office proposal moves.
    • The lot: the outline is drawn to the parcel map's lengths (70′ on the street, 59′ at the back, 118′ and 123′ down the sides) and squared to the house using your aerial photo. The driveway, front bed, patio and lawn are traced from the aerial at the house's own scale, good to roughly 3 feet. Which side is 118′ and which is 123′ is read from the parcel map's orientation.
    • Lights: the house ceiling lights, bath vanity lights, garage light and fireplace are placed from your photos. The second bedroom and foyer had no photo, so they have no light of their own. Sunlight comes from the back-yard side, where your photos show it landing.
    • Layout options: my reading of each designer's published rules applied to your rooms. They are suggestions to react to, not their work.
